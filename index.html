Mon 21 Dec · Day 1

Up the hill

  1. 04:50Reach NJP. Washrooms, chai, and a breather in the station. Check the live status, since trains often run late.
  2. 06:00Toto to Tenzing Norgay bus stand in Siliguri, about 25 min.
  3. 07:00NBSTC bus to Darjeeling. The 8:00 AM bus is the backup.
  4. ~10:00Reach Chowk Bazaar, then a taxi to Bubble Beds. Leave bags at reception and get brunch.
  5. 13:00Check in, freshen up, rest.
  6. EveningMahakal Temple on Observatory Hill, then Mall Road and Chowrasta. Book the cab for Day 2 and the Tiger Hill trip. Dinner.

Keep your photo ID handy for check-in (not PAN). Dawn at NJP and the bus ride are cold, so keep your jacket in your day bag, not the big bag.

Tue 22 Dec · Day 2

Carry cash for entry tickets and the ropeway. The ropeway queue gets long by late morning.

NJP → Darjeeling

Our train reaches NJP at 04:50, so there's time before the 7:00 AM bus. Budget: ₹2,000–2,500 for all six.

The road climbs almost 2,000 m in hairpin bends. Ghum, just before Darjeeling, is the highest point of the ride.

Government bus via Siliguri ₹1,050–1,700 Our pick₹175–285 eachabout 4 h
  • The bus leaves from Tenzing Norgay Central Bus Stand in Siliguri, not NJP. Take a toto from the station first: about 25 min and ₹200–500 for 6, maybe two totos.
  • 7:00 AM and 8:00 AM buses, arriving about 10:00 and 11:00. Non-AC seater via Rohini.
  • ₹105 a seat, checked on redBus and MakeMyTrip in Oct 2026. Only 4–5 seats per bus showed online, so book all 6 as soon as December opens.
  • It stops at Chowk Bazaar. Then 1–2 taxis to the hostel, about ₹200–500.
Shared jeep from NJP ₹2,000–2,900 Just about₹335–480 each3.5–5 h
  • Jeeps wait at the main taxi stand outside the station. Ask for "Darjeeling shared". The prepaid booth only sells whole cars.
  • About ₹300–400 a seat, up to ₹500 in peak December. Our limit is about ₹330 a seat. Agree the fare before bags go on the roof.
  • It leaves when full. Buying a 7th seat gives more room and gets us moving sooner.
  • Few jeeps run at 6 AM, so we may wait until about 7–7:30. Drops at the motor stand, not the hostel.
Reserved Sumo or Bolero ₹2,800–3,500 Over₹470–585 each3.5–4.5 h
  • A whole 8–10 seater, door to door, leaving right away. The prepaid booth charges about ₹3,500. Drivers outside sometimes go lower.
  • Tight with big bags.
Prepaid Innova ₹4,000–5,000 Over₹670–835 each3.5–4.5 h
  • The comfortable option, booked at the prepaid booth opposite the station exit. Keep the slip and check it says "Lebong Cart Road, below Zoo".
Pickup by Bubble Beds Ask Ask them3.5–4.5 h
  • Their tour package includes NJP pickup. If it's close to the Sumo fare, a driver waits for us when the train gets in.
Toy train – Not for arrivalabout 7.5 h
  • Leaves NJP at 10:00 AM and takes about 7.5 hours. Do the short Darjeeling–Ghum joyride (about ₹800) during the trip instead.

Totals are for all 6, door to door. Bus fares and times were checked on redBus and MakeMyTrip on 20 Oct 2026. Other prices are estimates from travel forums and 2025–26 guides.

The morning, if we take the bus
