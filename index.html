The options

1. Government bus (NBSTC) via Siliguri Our pick

₹1,050–1,700 for 6

The bus leaves from Tenzing Norgay Central Bus Stand in Siliguri, not from NJP. Take a toto from the station first.

  • Departures: 7:00 AM and 8:00 AM, arriving about 10:00 and 11:00 AM. This is the non-AC 2+2 seater via Rohini.
  • Fare: ₹105 a seat, checked on redBus and MakeMyTrip in Oct 2026. Online booking may add a small fee.
  • Getting to the bus: NJP to Tenzing Norgay by toto or auto takes about 25 min and costs about ₹200–500 for 6. You may need two totos.
  • From the Darjeeling bus stand: the bus stops at Chowk Bazaar. Taking 1–2 taxis to the hostel costs about ₹200–500.

Good

  • The cheapest by far
  • Leaves at a fixed time
  • You can book online ahead

Watch out

  • Only 4–5 seats per bus showed online. Book early or split across both buses
  • Limited room for big bags
  • One extra transfer at each end

2. Shared jeep from NJP

₹2,000–2,900 for 6

Jeeps leave from the main taxi stand just outside NJP station. Ask for "Darjeeling shared". Shared seats aren't sold at the prepaid booth, which is for whole cars.

  • Fare: about ₹300–400 a seat, and up to about ₹500 in peak December. The total above includes a taxi to the hostel.
  • Our limit: about ₹330 a seat. Above that, the jeep alone costs more than ₹2,000 for 6. Agree the fare before bags go on the roof.
  • The jeep leaves only when it's full (about 10–11 people). We fill 6 seats, so it should go faster. Buying a 7th seat gives more room and can get us moving sooner.
  • Bags go on the roof under a tarp.
  • It drops us at the Darjeeling motor stand, not the hostel.

Good

  • Straight from NJP with no transfer
  • A bit quicker than the bus

Watch out

  • Few jeeps run at 6 AM, so we may wait until about 7–7:30
  • Cramped
  • Fix the fare before loading bags

3. Reserved Sumo or Bolero

₹2,800–3,500 for 6

A whole 8–10 seater for the group. The prepaid booth charges about ₹3,500, and drivers outside sometimes go lower if we bargain.

Good

  • Door to door
  • Leaves right away at 6 AM
  • We can stop for chai and photos

Watch out

  • Over budget
  • Tight with big bags

4. Prepaid Innova

₹4,000–5,000 for 6

The comfortable option. Book at the prepaid taxi booth opposite the station exit, which reportedly runs 24 hours. Fares were about ₹4,000 in 2025 and will likely be higher in December.

Good

  • Most comfortable, with room for luggage
  • Fixed fare on a printed slip

Watch out

  • About double our budget

5. Pickup arranged by Bubble Beds

Ask them

The hostel's tour package includes pickup from NJP, Siliguri or Bagdogra. WhatsApp +91 81000 94453 for a price. If it's close to the Sumo fare, the driver waits for us and knows the way.

6. Toy train Skip for arrival

–

It leaves NJP at 10:00 AM and takes about 7.5 hours, and it gets suspended after landslides. Do the short Darjeeling–Ghum joyride (about ₹800) during the trip instead.

The plan if we take the bus

  1. 6:00Arrive at NJP. Grab chai and don't talk to touts inside the station.
  2. 6:15Toto or auto to Tenzing Norgay Central Bus Stand in Siliguri (about 25 min). Agree the fare first.
  3. 7:00Take the NBSTC bus to Darjeeling. The 8:00 AM bus is the relaxed backup.
  4. 10–11Reach the Darjeeling bus stand at Chowk Bazaar. Take a taxi to Bubble Beds, below the zoo.
  5. 1:00 PMCheck in. Until then, leave bags at reception and go eat.

Good to know

  • Bus seats open about 3 weeks ahead on redBus and MakeMyTrip. December dates aren't open yet, so check in late November and book all 6 together.
  • Roads: Rohini Road is the fastest. Hill Cart Road via Kurseong is the slower backup, and Pankhabari Road is in bad shape. Ask the hostel the day before which road is open.
  • December mornings are foggy and cold (about 2–10°C), so keep a jacket in your hand luggage.
  • Prepaid booth: keep the slip and check that it says "Lebong Cart Road, below Zoo". Don't pay the driver extra.
  • The direct NJP–Darjeeling NBSTC bus (listed at about 8:30 AM) doesn't show online. Ask at NJP whether it's running, because it would skip the Siliguri transfer.

Bus fares and times were checked on redBus and MakeMyTrip on 20 Oct 2026. Toto, taxi, jeep and SUV prices are estimates from travel forums and 2025–26 guides, so confirm them on the day.
