Totals include getting to the hostel door. Tap an option below for the details.

Government bus (NBSTC) via Siliguri Our pick₹1,050–1,700
  • The bus leaves from Tenzing Norgay Central Bus Stand in Siliguri, not NJP. Take a toto from the station first (about 25 min, ₹200–500 for 6, maybe two totos).
  • 7:00 AM and 8:00 AM buses, arriving about 10:00 and 11:00. Non-AC seater via Rohini.
  • ₹105 a seat, checked on redBus and MakeMyTrip in Oct 2026. Only 4–5 seats per bus showed online, so book all 6 as soon as December opens.
  • It stops at Chowk Bazaar. Then 1–2 taxis to the hostel, about ₹200–500.
Shared jeep from NJP₹2,000–2,900
  • Jeeps wait at the main taxi stand outside the station. Ask for "Darjeeling shared". The prepaid booth only sells whole cars.
  • About ₹300–400 a seat, up to ₹500 in peak December. Our limit is about ₹330 a seat. Agree the fare before bags go on the roof.
  • It leaves when full. Buying a 7th seat gives more room and gets us moving sooner.
  • Few jeeps run at 6 AM, so we may wait until about 7–7:30. Drops at the motor stand, not the hostel.
Reserved Sumo or Bolero₹2,800–3,500
  • A whole 8–10 seater, door to door, leaving right away. The prepaid booth charges about ₹3,500. Drivers outside sometimes go lower.
  • Tight with big bags.
Prepaid Innova₹4,000–5,000
  • The comfortable option, booked at the prepaid booth opposite the station exit. Keep the slip and check it says "Lebong Cart Road, below Zoo".
Pickup arranged by Bubble BedsAsk them
  • Their tour package includes NJP pickup. If it's close to the Sumo fare, a driver waits for us at 6 AM.
Toy train Skip for arrival–
  • Leaves NJP at 10:00 AM and takes about 7.5 hours. Do the short Darjeeling–Ghum joyride (about ₹800) during the trip instead.

The morning, if we take the bus

  1. 6:00Arrive at NJP. Grab chai and ignore touts inside the station.
  2. 6:15Toto or auto to Tenzing Norgay bus stand. Agree the fare first.
  3. 7:00NBSTC bus to Darjeeling. The 8:00 AM bus is the backup.
  4. 10–11Reach Chowk Bazaar. Taxi to Bubble Beds, below the zoo.
  5. 1:00 PMCheck in. Until then, leave bags at reception and go eat.

Bring

  • Government photo ID for everyone: Aadhaar, passport, voter ID or driving licence. PAN isn't accepted.
  • One person's ID stays at reception until check-out, since we're a group of 6.

House rules

  • No smoking, alcohol, food or loud talk in dorms. Quiet after 11 PM.
  • Visitors: max 2, 9 AM–7 PM, common area only.
  • Little or no power backup during cuts.

On site

  • Bubble Cafe for breakfast (8–10:30 AM), lunch and dinner. Order at reception.
  • Terrace, common room with board games, lockers, laundry.
  • Tour desk for Tiger Hill and day trips.

Cancellation

  • Groups of 4+: free if cancelled 30+ days before.
  • 50% between 30 and 15 days, full charge within 15 days.
